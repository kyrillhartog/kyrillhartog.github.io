About

Journalist turned content strategist

I started out as a journalist and co-founded Are We Europe, an award-winning magazine I led as editor-in-chief. I then co-founded Awe Studio, a strategic communications studio for NGOs, foundations and international organisations, with campaigns for clients such as Greenpeace and Amnesty. Since then I've worked with more than 25 companies, from early-stage startups to multinationals, setting the editorial voice and standard for their most important content.

My work sits at the intersection of content, brand strategy and executive storytelling. Whether it's launching a category, educating a market or helping founders explain complex ideas, I write content that earns attention, trust and long-term authority.

Today I also lead brand and content at DUPR, the sports-tech company behind pickleball's global rating system, where we've reached #1 Google rankings for the sport's most valuable search terms and built a 1.3M-subscriber newsletter. I serve on Malt's freelancer advisory board.
